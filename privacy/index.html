CountCal Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026
Operator: YT
Contact: finnertam@gmail.com

CountCal respects your privacy. This policy explains how CountCal 1.0.2 handles information.

Data Handling

CountCal doesn’t require an account, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or track your activity across other apps or websites.

The trackers, date records, quotas, and reminder rules you create are stored in the app container on your iPhone. CountCal doesn’t upload this content to developer servers. Device backups may include app data, depending on your iPhone and iCloud backup settings.

Notifications

CountCal requests notification permission only when you save a tracker with reminders enabled or tap Enable Notifications in Settings. Local notifications are scheduled by iOS. Turning off notifications doesn’t affect date records or statistics.

Notifications use generic content by default and don’t show tracker names or quotas. You can choose to show tracker details in Settings. iOS controls notification previews on the Lock Screen and in Notification Center.

Retention and Deletion

Data remains on the device until you delete a tracker, delete all on-device data in Settings, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; offloading the app may retain documents and data. Copies in device backups are managed by your system backup settings.

Delete All Data clears trackers, date records, reminder rules, app preferences, and CountCal notifications. It doesn’t change notification permission or device backups managed by iOS.

Data Export

You can export your on-device data as a readable JSON file. CountCal creates a copy only when you choose to export. You control where the file is saved or shared and how it is handled afterward.

Third Parties and Network Services

CountCal doesn’t sell or share your trackers or date records with third parties. The current version has no account system, cloud sync, third-party advertising, or analytics services.

If you voluntarily contact support by email, the operator processes the information you provide only to respond and resolve the issue.

This privacy policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit these pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the service, according to the GitHub General Privacy Statement. YT does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies to these pages.

Policy Updates

If CountCal’s data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated and, when appropriate, explained in the app or on its product page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to finnertam@gmail.com.

点历隐私政策

生效日期：2026-08-14
运营者：YT
联系邮箱：finnertam@gmail.com

点历重视你的隐私。本政策说明点历 1.0.2 如何处理信息。

数据处理

点历不要求账号，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也不会跟踪你在其他 App 或网站中的活动。

你创建的项目、日期记录、配额和提醒规则保存在 iPhone 的 App 容器中。点历不会把这些内容上传到开发者服务器。系统设备备份可能包含 App 数据，具体取决于你的 iPhone 与 iCloud 备份设置。

通知

只有当你保存已开启提醒的项目，或在设置中点按“开启通知”时，点历才会请求通知权限。本地通知由 iOS 安排。关闭通知权限不会影响日期记录和统计功能。

通知默认使用通用内容，不显示项目名称或额度。你可以在设置中选择显示项目详情；锁屏和通知中心的通知预览方式由 iOS 系统设置控制。

数据保留与删除

数据会保留在设备上，直到你删除项目、在设置中删除全部本机数据，或删除 App。删除 App 会移除本机 App 数据；卸载 App 可能保留文稿与数据。设备备份中的副本由系统备份设置管理。

“删除全部数据”会清除项目、日期记录、提醒规则、App 偏好和点历通知，但不会更改由 iOS 管理的通知权限或设备备份。

数据导出

你可以把本机数据导出为可读的 JSON 文件。点历只会在你主动导出后创建副本；文件的保存位置、共享目的地及其后续处理由你选择和管理。

第三方与网络服务

点历不会向第三方出售或共享你的项目与日期记录。当前版本不包含登录、云同步、第三方广告或分析服务。

如果你通过支持邮箱主动发送信息，运营者只会为回复与解决问题而处理你提供的内容。

本隐私政策及支持页面托管于 GitHub Pages。访问这些页面时，GitHub 可能会依照其 GitHub 通用隐私声明，处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息和请求日志等技术信息，以提供并保障托管服务。YT 未在这些页面中添加分析、广告或跟踪 Cookie。

政策更新

如果点历的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策会同步更新，并在适用时通过 App 或产品页说明。
