Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Trackers and date records are stored locally by the app and aren’t uploaded to a developer server. The current version has no account or cloud sync; system backups may include app data, depending on your iPhone and iCloud settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an individual tracker from its editor, or choose Delete All Data in Settings. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; offloading may retain documents and data. Copies in backups are managed by your system backup settings.

How do I export a copy of my data?

Open Settings, choose Data Management, then Export a Copy of Your Data. CountCal creates a readable JSON file and lets you choose where to save or share it.

Why didn’t a reminder arrive?

Check notification status in CountCal Settings and confirm that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings. Focus modes and Notification Summary may also affect delivery time.

Notifications hide tracker names and quotas by default. You can change this in CountCal Settings; Lock Screen previews are controlled separately by iOS.

Can totals be used as tax or visa evidence?

No. CountCal provides personal records only and isn’t proof of tax, visa, medical, or employment compliance.

常见问题

数据保存在哪里？

项目与日期记录由 App 保存在这台 iPhone 上，不上传至开发者服务器。当前版本不提供账号或云同步；系统备份可能按你的 iPhone 与 iCloud 设置包含 App 数据。

如何删除数据？

你可以编辑并删除单个项目，也可以在“设置”中选择“删除全部数据”。删除 App 会移除本机 App 数据；卸载 App 可能保留文稿与数据。备份中的副本由系统备份设置管理。

如何导出数据副本？

前往“设置”→“数据管理”→“导出数据副本”。点历会生成可读的 JSON 文件，并让你选择保存或共享位置。

为什么没有收到提醒？

请在点历“设置”中检查通知状态，并在 iOS 系统设置中确认允许通知。专注模式和通知摘要也可能影响送达时间。

通知默认隐藏项目名称和额度。你可以在点历“设置”中调整；锁屏预览由 iOS 另行控制。

日期统计能否作为税务或签证证明？

不能。点历只提供个人记录与参考，不构成税务、签证、医疗或雇佣合规证明。
